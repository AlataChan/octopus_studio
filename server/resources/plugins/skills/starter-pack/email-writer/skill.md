# Email Writing (Multiple Tones/Scenarios)

This skill helps you quickly write emails that are clear in purpose, polite in tone, and effective at moving work forward.
You provide the recipient relationship (customer, colleague, or vendor), email purpose, background information, expected action from the recipient, and deadline.
I first output the key points to make sure the logic is clear, then generate the full email body, including a subject suggestion and call to action (CTA) by default.
You can choose different tones: formal, friendly, firm but polite, concise, or detailed, and I will adjust wording and structure accordingly.
It also supports writing between English and other languages while keeping expression natural rather than literal.

## Common Scenarios

- Requirement clarification, meeting invitations, and follow-up reminders
- Change notices, delay communication, and risk alerts
- Partnership quotations and confirmation of contract terms
