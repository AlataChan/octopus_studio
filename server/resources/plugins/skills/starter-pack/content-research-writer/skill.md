# Content Research Writing (Research to Outline to Draft)

This skill is suitable for writing articles, white papers, special reports, or external-facing content that needs evidence, structure, and a clear point of view.
You provide the topic, target readers, publishing channel, and length requirements, and I first provide a search strategy and keyword combinations to quickly cover mainstream viewpoints and key data.
The research phase outputs a source list, summaries of core conclusions, and points that still require further verification, preventing uncertain information from entering the body text.
Then I generate a reviewable outline with section goals and supporting evidence, and after your confirmation, produce a first draft with coherent logic and consistent expression.
If you want it to resemble a particular style, I can also adjust tone and pacing without changing the facts.

## Use Cases

- Entry-level research and quick drafting for a new field
- Market or competitor analysis content output
- Structured production of long-form writing
