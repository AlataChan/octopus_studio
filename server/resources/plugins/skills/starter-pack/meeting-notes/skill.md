# Meeting Notes (Action Items/Decisions/Risks)

This skill turns a meeting where many things were discussed into notes that can drive follow-up work after the meeting.
You can provide an audio transcript, chat log, handwritten bullets, or a spoken summary, and I will first extract the themes and agenda order.
Then I organize everything into a consistent template: conclusions and decisions, action items (owner + deadline + acceptance criteria), risks and dependencies, and open questions.
If disagreements appear in the meeting, I present the different viewpoints side by side and suggest what information to add or how to align next time.
The output is ready by default for copy-paste into Lark, Notion, Confluence, and similar tools, and can include an external-facing version that hides internal sensitive details.

## Recommended Input

- Attendee list and roles (optional)
- Meeting goal in one sentence
- The three questions you care about most (optional)
