# Resume Optimization (Job Matching/Rewriting)

This skill turns a resume with many experiences but little focus into a resume that clearly matches the target role at first glance.
You provide the target job description, current resume (bullet points are enough), and the direction you want to highlight, such as management, technology, growth, or operations.
I first extract the role profile and keywords, then map them to your experience: what to emphasize, what to downplay, and where quantified metrics should be added.
Then I output replaceable paragraph rewrites, including project descriptions, responsibilities, and results, plus likely interview follow-up points and preparation suggestions.
If you need different versions, such as for large companies, startups, or overseas applications, I adjust the emphasis and tone separately.

## Common Optimization Points

- Use numbers to show outcomes: how much improved, how much saved, or how many users affected
- Use consistent verbs and structure: owned, drove, delivered, optimized
- Make each experience map directly to job requirements
