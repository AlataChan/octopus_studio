# Brand Guidelines Assistant (Copy/Visual Consistency)

When multiple people on a team write copy, make posters, or create product introductions, the most common problems are inconsistent style and imprecise expression.
You only need to provide the brand positioning in one sentence, target users, competitor comparisons, and any existing examples if available.
I will produce a practical draft of brand guidelines, including brand voice, preferred expressions, prohibited expressions, and punctuation and formatting habits.
If visual guidance is involved, I will turn requirements for colors, fonts, spacing, icon style, and related elements into a checklist for quick self-review by design and operations teams.
I also provide 3-5 rewriting examples so the team can immediately follow the style in both writing and execution.

## Use Cases

- A new product or business launch needs a clear tone quickly
- Multi-channel campaigns and content matrices need unified messaging
- A brand upgrade or visual system change is in its migration period
