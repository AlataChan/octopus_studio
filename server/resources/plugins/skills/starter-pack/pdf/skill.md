# PDF Office (Extraction/Summary/Structuring)

This skill is suitable for PDFs that are information-dense but hard to edit, such as reports, manuals, bid documents, and policy files.
You provide the PDF file and state your focus areas, such as conclusions, clauses, timelines, costs, or risks, and I prioritize extracting relevant paragraphs and key points according to those areas.
When summarizing, I distinguish original facts from my interpretation or inference, and list the parts that need secondary verification to reduce the risk of misreading.
For long documents, I first output the table of contents and chapter summaries, then extract and structure specific chapters in depth as requested.
Output options include key-point lists, chapter summaries, Q&A-style quick reads, or structured fields that can be pasted into a spreadsheet.

## Use Cases

- Quickly understand long reports or manuals
- Extract key obligations and deadlines from contracts or clauses
- Prepare a reading brief and risk alert for the team
