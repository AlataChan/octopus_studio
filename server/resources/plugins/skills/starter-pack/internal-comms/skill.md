# Internal Communication Writing (Weekly Reports/Announcements/Postmortems)

This skill focuses on turning scattered information into internal communication that teams can understand and act on.
You provide materials such as bullet points, data, timelines, links, or chat logs, and I first extract the conclusion and key numbers before organizing them into a fixed structure.
For weekly reports, I highlight progress, blockers, next week's plan, and support needed; for announcements, I clarify impact scope, changes, and action guidance.
For postmortems, I use a facts to impact to root cause to action items framework, avoiding blame and vague reflection while producing trackable improvements.
The output includes versions of different lengths (concise, standard, and detailed) so it can fit different channels.

## Materials You Can Provide

- Items completed and not completed this week, plus key metrics
- Decision background, impact scope, and external commitments
- Incident timeline, symptoms, and actions already taken
